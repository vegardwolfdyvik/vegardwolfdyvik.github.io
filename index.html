About

Of Norway is the joint force of Vegard Wolf-Dyvik & Carl Christian Steenstrup.

For the last 20 years they have been releasing music, remixing, playing live and DJing around the world. With over 100 releases for labels such as Connaisseur, Last Night On Earth, Bedrock, Flores, Sum Over Histories, DNSOTF, Full Pupp, Suara, Super Nature (etc etc)

Their eclectic approach to house, disco, electro and anything in between, has taken their live act and DJsets to play clubs and festivals such as Lab.Oratory/Berghain (GER), Fabric (UK), Fusion Festival (GER), Miami Music Conference (US), DNSOTF (US), ADE (NL), Bar25 (GER), Tresor (GER), Katerblau (GER), Jaeger (NO), Øya Festivalen (NO) to mention a mere few.
